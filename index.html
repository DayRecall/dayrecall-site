How it works

One tap in the morning. Answers all day.

1Remember what was said

Your Apple Watch listens during the hours you choose, even with your iPhone left behind. One tap in the morning, and it stops on its own. Our in-house ultra-light speech model runs right on the watch and keeps only the moments when someone is talking.

2Organized for you

Speech is turned into text on this iPhone, and the audio is cleared once transcribed. Your chosen AI engine finds to-dos, deadlines and meetings. It can also tell what you said from what others said.

3Private by default

By default, this iPhone finds to-dos, writes recaps and answers questions. If you choose and allow your own API, new transcripts are sent automatically for to-dos, a day’s conversations when you request a recap, and questions with matching excerpts. Audio is never sent to the API.

Devices

Your watch doesn’t need your phone nearby.

Apple Watch listens on its own, all day. What it hears waits on the watch and goes to your iPhone every two hours, and if your iPhone isn’t around, it catches up as soon as the two reconnect, say when you get home in the evening. Your iPhone never has to stay connected.

Battery

Listens all day. Barely touches the battery.

A professional-grade, ultra-light speech model built in-house: just 2,561 parameters, running on our own accelerated inference kernels and making a call every tenth of a second, right on the watch.

Only speech is kept. Silence, keyboards and fans are filtered out before they cost any battery. Everything from the architecture to the kernels was designed for the watch, drawing on our doctoral research in AI acceleration.

Voices

Knows your voice. Tells everyone else apart.

Read five short cards once, about a minute, and everything you say is marked Me. Everyone else in the conversation gets a number of their own, Other 1, Other 2 and on, so your promises and the ones made to you never get mixed up.

  • Several speakers in one conversation, each told apart
  • To-dos know whose promise it was
  • Speaker recognition for an hour of talk in 4.4 seconds
  • Your voiceprint stays on your iPhone. Other voices are never stored.

Optional and off by default. Speed based on internal testing on iPhone 17 Pro Max.

Languages

Switch languages mid-conversation. It keeps up.

Pick up to three working languages. DayRecall detects every switch and transcribes each part in the language it was spoken in. Lines in the other languages can then be translated into yours.

  • Up to three languages in one conversation
  • Each part transcribed in its own language
  • Translated into your language
  • Language identification for an hour of talk in about 32 seconds

Multilingual transcription is an advanced setting, off by default. Speed based on internal testing on iPhone 17 Pro Max. The app itself comes in 14 languages.

Health

Sometimes you remember by what you were doing

Turn it on and DayRecall reads your sleep, walks, workouts and heart rate from the Health app. They become clues on your day: a conversation can say it happened while you were walking, just after a workout, or with a heart rate faster than usual.

  • Labels on your timeline: walking, working out, asleep, heart rate above usual
  • Ask “What did we talk about before bed?” or “What did I say while I was running?”
  • Off until you turn it on. Read only: DayRecall never writes to Health.
  • Read only. Health information is kept in a protected cache on this iPhone and is not sent to us. Health labels and values go to your API or an AI app only if you choose to add them to a recap or shared day. Adding Health data is off by default.

FAQ

Questions, answered

All questions→

What is DayRecall?

DayRecall is an AI memory app for Apple Watch and iPhone, made for people who forget things. During the hours you choose, it listens, keeps what was said as text on your iPhone, and answers questions about your day with the exact words and where they came from.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. DayRecall works best with Apple Watch, and your iPhone can also listen on its own. When both listen, your day is one timeline, and what both heard is shown once.

Does DayRecall keep audio recordings?

No. What’s said is turned into text on your iPhone, and the audio is cleared automatically once it’s transcribed. Only the text is kept.

Where is my data, and who can see it?

Your data is stored on your iPhone and in backups you choose to make. There is no account and no DayRecall server receiving your day. To-dos, recaps and answers use this iPhone by default. If you select and allow your own API, it receives new transcripts automatically for to-dos, a day’s conversations when you request a recap, and questions with matching excerpts. It may first receive a question alone to help search. You can also share a selected day with an AI app. These recipients use their own privacy rules; audio is never sent to the API.
