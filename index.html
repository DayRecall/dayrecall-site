How it works

One tap in the morning. Answers all day.

1Remember what was said

Your Apple Watch listens during the hours you choose. One tap in the morning, and it stops on its own. A small speech model of our own runs right on the watch and keeps only the moments when someone is talking.

2Organized for you

Your iPhone turns the speech into text on the device, deletes the audio, and picks out the to-dos, deadlines and meetings. It can also tell what you said from what others said.

3Private by default

Your memories stay on your iPhone, and answers use Apple’s on-device model. You can choose your own cloud API later; nothing is sent until you allow it.

Features

Made for the details you’d otherwise lose

Who said what

Record your voice once, and your lines are marked Me. Everyone else is Other 1, Other 2. So “I’ll send it Friday” is clearly your promise. Optional, and your voiceprint never leaves your iPhone.

To-dos you confirm

Promises, deadlines and meetings are picked out of your day. Nothing reaches your calendar or Reminders until you say yes.

Ask in your own words

What did Sam promise? When is the report due? Every answer shows where it came from, down to the line that was said.

Any day, at a glance

See a day as a timeline, flip back through past days, and search everything you’ve heard.

A plan for the week

Each week, the deadlines and promises ahead in one place. You pick what goes on your calendar.

Places, if you want them

See where each conversation happened on a map of your day. Off until you turn it on.

Hand a day to your AI app

For a deeper look, send a day’s transcript to the AI app you use, as a text file, only when you choose.

In 13 languages

DayRecall comes in 13 languages, and it can follow a conversation that switches between them.

Privacy

Text only. On your iPhone. Your call.

Text, not audio

Speech is turned into text on your iPhone, and then the audio is deleted.

No account, no server of ours

There’s nothing to sign up for, and we don’t run servers that see your day.

Nothing leaves without you

Answers use Apple’s on-device model. Handing a day to an AI app, or using your own cloud API, happens only when you allow it.

Tell the people around you

Tell the people you talk with that DayRecall is listening. Some places require everyone’s consent even when only text is kept, so follow the rules where you are.
